**Casting Time:** 1 action (or reaction when a broom looks at you funny)  
**Range:** Personal  
**Components:** V, S, M (a broken broom, optional but therapeutic)  
**Duration:** 10 minutes (or until the broom stops hating you)

### Description
By casting this spell, the caster invokes a mystical barrier specifically designed to defend them from the pent-up wrath of cleaning tools.

For the duration of the spell:
- You gain **resistance** to bludgeoning damage from **brooms, dustpans, brushes, mops**, and any object whose purpose in life was to *clean*.
- **Animated brooms** have disadvantage on attack rolls against you, because they vaguely remind them of the time you were *“badly beaten.”*
- If you are a **Leonin**, you can add your **proficiency bonus** to Wisdom saving throws against public humiliation.


In addition, the first time a broom hits you while the spell is active, you can use your reaction to stare at it intensely, causing it to momentarily lose the will to live (it is **frightened** until the start of its next turn).

### Leveling Up

When you cast this spell using a 2nd-level or higher slot, you can also include:
- Buckets
- Dishcloths
- Any household item that “can't hurt anyway”

### Trivia
According to some arcane scholars, the spell does not create a true magical barrier, but convinces brooms that **it's not worth it**. It works surprisingly often.

The first version of the spell only protected against brooms. It was updated after a **particularly motivated mop** nearly caused a TPK.

The Leonin claim that the spell works better if you growl at the closet while casting it. No scientific proof. Just respect.

In at least three wizard towers, “Protection from Brooms and Mops” is classified as a **home security spell** and is mandatory for anyone living alone with suspicious furniture.

This spell exists because *someone* decided that confronting an animated broom was a good idea... **It was not.**
